## Exact full-text parent check

Eckert, Klumpp & Su (2017) was reopened at full-text level because its abstract is particularly close.

The paper states that its baseline firms choose between two investment levels, emphasizes lumpy investment, and assumes baseline downstream Cournot quantities remain positive for every relevant marginal-cost pair. Its extensions allow more discrete investment levels.

Therefore it does not directly contain the source correction's continuous sourcing / downstream-active-set mechanism.
