## Corrected mechanism statement

The project now distinguishes:

1. **own-payoff concavity versus equilibrium uniqueness:** globally strict concavity in own sourcing does not make the two-player best-response operator a contraction;
2. **all-active multiplicity:** when the regular active best response is sufficiently steep, the sourcing cap can create the asymmetric pair even though both firms remain active everywhere;
3. **exit-induced BR reversal:** when the cap is sufficiently large, the downstream exit threshold creates the positive-slope (U(y)=delta+2y) best-response piece;
4. **global-continuation necessity:** downstream participation changes must still be solved because they are feasible in the source model and invalidate a global strategic-substitutes interpretation, but they are not necessary for every multiplicity result.

No new generic multiplicity mechanism is claimed.
