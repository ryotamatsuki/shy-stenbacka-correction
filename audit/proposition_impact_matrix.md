## Rules

1. Do not label a proposition “wrong” merely because a proof step is incomplete.
2. Distinguish:
   - proof defect with true result;
   - omitted equilibrium;
   - incorrect uniqueness claim;
   - incorrect comparative-static claim;
   - incorrect welfare/market-structure implication.
3. For every affected result, provide a minimal counterexample or exact parameter region where possible.
4. Track whether the Hotelling-side correction independently changes the same proposition.
