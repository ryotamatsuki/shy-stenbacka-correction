# Derivations

Clean analytical derivations live here after they have been checked against the published model.

Recommended split:

- `outsourcing_stage.md` — constrained outsourcing optimization and boundary cases.
- `hotelling_stage.md` — location/price/demand conditions and deviations.
- `boundary_cases.md` — equality cases, full outsourcing, and other corners.

Raw scratch work should not be treated as canonical. Once a derivation is relied on in the manuscript, it should be independently rechecked and linked from the relevant audit file.
