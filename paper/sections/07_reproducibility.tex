\section*{Statements and Declarations}

\paragraph{Data availability.}
This is a theoretical study.  No empirical dataset was generated or analyzed for the results reported in the paper.

\paragraph{Code availability.}
The symbolic and numerical verification scripts, the figure generator, theorem-audit records, and the proof-assistant source used for the proof-critical formalization are maintained in the project repository at
\url{https://github.com/ryotamatsuki/shy-stenbacka-correction}.
The formal verification covers selected proof-critical statements rather than the complete economic model.

\paragraph{Use of generative AI.}
Generative-AI tools were used as research-assistance tools for drafting, code assistance, literature-search support, and adversarial checking during manuscript development.  The mathematical claims, citations, interpretation, and final manuscript remain subject to human review and responsibility.
